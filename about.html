Our Story & Mission

Trendinovia Investment Limited is a multi-sector enterprise dedicated to delivering world-class solutions tailored to the growing ecosystem of Papua New Guinea.

Why Trendinovia?

We believe in divergent growth—expanding our mastery across distinct industries to shield our operations from market volatility while maximizing shared value for partners, corporate clients, and communities alike.

Operating out of our central office in Port Moresby, our dedicated branch divisions are equipped with specialized teams focused heavily on quality infrastructure delivery, technical asset procurement, and responsive public utility management.

Corporate Pillars

  • Integrity: Upholding structural accountability across all multi-layered procurement tenders.
  • Innovation: Implementing state-of-the-art technological tools and methodologies.
  • Localization: Committed to building internal institutional capabilities right here in PNG.
